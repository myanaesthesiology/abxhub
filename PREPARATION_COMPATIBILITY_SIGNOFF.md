# Injectable preparation / compatibility sign-off — v0.18.0

## Source architecture

1. **Primary standard preparation:** MOH Dilution Guideline for Injectable Drugs (Part I – Antimicrobial).
2. **Fluid-restricted ICU overlay:** MOH Dilution Guide for Fluid Restricted Critically Ill Adults, Dec 2025.
3. Sabah protocol / product label: fallback only where the national source does not cover the product or a formulation-specific detail is required.

## What has been validated structurally

AbxHub keeps reconstitution, further dilution, compatible diluent, administration, stability and compatibility fields distinct. The fluid-restricted concentration is never used as a general default.

## What still requires local pharmacy sign-off

For every injectable—and especially high-risk formulations—confirm the locally stocked product and:

- vial strength / formulation;
- reconstitution solvent;
- final concentration;
- compatible diluent;
- infusion rate/duration;
- post-reconstitution and post-dilution stability;
- Y-site / line compatibility where clinically relevant;
- storage/light-protection requirements;
- maximum concentration used for fluid restriction.

Priority local sign-off: amphotericin B formulations, vancomycin, colistin/CMS, polymyxin B, IV co-trimoxazole, acyclovir, piperacillin-tazobactam and meropenem.
