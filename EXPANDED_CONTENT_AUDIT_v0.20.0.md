## Specific depth corrections

- CRAB retains the dedicated HPUSM/MSIDC sulbactam preparation and renal/RRT table in addition to the generic resistance-management structure.
- ESBL now includes uncomplicated cystitis, pyelonephritis/cUTI, outside-urinary-tract and severe/ICU distinctions, with NAG quick-guide linkage and MSIDC cautions around definitive piperacillin-tazobactam/cefepime use.
- AmpC now separates moderate-risk inducible organisms from lower-risk organisms and adds urinary vs extra-urinary strategy, cefepime/carbapenem context and the piperacillin-tazobactam caution.
- CRE/CPE now separates non-carbapenemase CRE, carbapenemase-producing CRE, NDM/MBL, KPC and OXA-48-like pathways and surfaces urinary vs non-urinary strategy plus susceptibility/mechanism guards.
- MDR/DTR Pseudomonas now distinguishes traditional beta-lactam-susceptible MDR disease from DTR disease and separates cystitis, pyelonephritis/cUTI and extra-urinary infection.
- Stenotrophomonas now explicitly starts with infection-vs-colonisation and adds source-specific TMP-SMX/newer-agent/combination considerations.
- PJI now exposes the bundled HPUSM retained-implant MSSA/MRSA regimens, rifampicin role and 14–42-day table duration while preserving surgery/culture dependence.
- The invasive-fungal reference hub now exposes the actual MSIC voriconazole loading/maintenance and renal-formulation guidance rather than only pointing users back to the PDF.

## Validation rules

The expanded-content validator rejects unknown source IDs, unknown linked drug IDs, broken organism pathway targets, missing duration rows, resistance site rows without curated drug links, and the CRAB formulation-component false-link regression (plain ampicillin must not be surfaced as a standalone CRAB treatment link).
