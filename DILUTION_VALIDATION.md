# Dilution / preparation validation — v0.15.0

## Source hierarchy
1. MOH Dilution Guideline for Injectable Drugs Part I — Antimicrobial: standard preparation baseline where structured.
2. MOH Dilution Guide for Fluid Restricted Critically Ill Adults, Dec 2025: separate minimum-volume ICU overlay when clinically necessary.
3. Other Malaysian/local/product sources: explicit fallback only when a national entry is unavailable or formulation-specific information is required.

### Formulation guard
The MOH guide contains amphotericin B **lipid complex**, while AbxHub currently contains **liposomal amphotericin B**. They are different formulations. The lipid-complex preparation record is therefore deliberately not attached to the liposomal monograph.

Dilution, stability and compatibility remain brand/formulation specific. Verify the locally stocked product insert and local pharmacy policy before administration.
