# AbxHub clinical review queue — pre-v1.0 after v0.15 source reconciliation

The following are **institutional sign-off tasks**, not necessarily source-extraction gaps.

## Priority 1 — local TDM workflows
1. Vancomycin: validate local Bayesian/two-level AUC method, assay turnaround, sampling timing and documentation workflow.
2. Gentamicin/amikacin: validate the local EID/conventional nomogram, level timing, AKI/dialysis handling and paediatric workflow.

## Priority 2 — highest medication-error risk
3. Colistin/CMS: confirm local vial strength and whether prescribing is in million IU, CMS mass or colistin-base activity; validate HD/SLED/CRRT rules.
4. Polymyxin B: confirm local vial strength, loading/maintenance practice, toxicity monitoring and preparation.
5. Amphotericin B: validate deoxycholate vs lipid-complex vs liposomal formulation naming, stock and preparation; never transfer instructions between formulations.

## Priority 3 — ICU PK/PD / RRT
6. Validate CRRT effluent-rate assumptions and residual renal function for beta-lactams, vancomycin, aminoglycosides and newer MDR agents.
7. Validate prolonged-infusion stability/compatibility with locally stocked cefepime, ceftazidime, imipenem/cilastatin, meropenem and piperacillin/tazobactam.
8. Confirm ARC handling, particularly cefiderocol and high-clearance beta-lactam patients.

## Priority 4 — MDR specialist regimens
9. Ceftazidime-avibactam + aztreonam: confirm local availability, simultaneous administration/Y-site workflow and renal adjustment for both components.
10. Cefiderocol: confirm local access, infusion, renal/ARC and organism/site-specific role.
11. High-dose tigecycline: confirm site limitations and bacteraemia caveat.

## Priority 5 — paediatric / neonatal
12. Line-by-line review of Blue Book vs Frank Shann dose fields by age, gestation, indication, maximum dose and renal function.
13. Do not scale adult CRRT regimens into children without paediatric-specific validation.

## Priority 6 — empirical therapy
14. Align HPUSM empirical pathways to the current local antibiogram before institutional deployment.
15. Prioritise HAP/VAP, septic shock, febrile neutropenia, meningitis, complicated intra-abdominal infection and urosepsis.

## Review method
For each signed-off record: open the linked original source; confirm formulation/population/indication; confirm loading and maintenance dose, route, interval and infusion; verify renal/RRT modality; verify paediatric age/gestation basis where relevant; verify preparation against stocked product; record reviewer initials/date; only then mark `clinicalReview: REVIEWED`.
