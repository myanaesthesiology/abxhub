## High-yield domains now represented

- HAP/VAP, severe CAP, aspiration pneumonia, lung abscess/empyema and COPD exacerbation.
- Meningitis/brain abscess plus HSV encephalitis, spinal epidural abscess, healthcare-associated ventriculitis and cryptococcal meningitis.
- UTI/pyelonephritis/urosepsis plus acute/chronic bacterial prostatitis and Fournier gangrene.
- Intra-abdominal infection, SBP, diverticulitis, appendicitis and infected pancreatic necrosis.
- Febrile neutropenia, PCP/PJP, toxoplasma encephalitis, histoplasmosis and MAC.
- Melioidosis, leptospirosis, typhoid, cholera, rickettsial disease, brucellosis, tetanus and severe malaria.
- ESBL, AmpC, CRE, non-CP CRE, KPC, NDM/MBL, OXA-48-like, CRAB, MDR/DTR Pseudomonas and Stenotrophomonas.
- Expanded novel-agent/critical-care renal data from the supplied MSIC/MSIDC sources.
