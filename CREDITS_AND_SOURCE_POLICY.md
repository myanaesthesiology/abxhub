# Source and redistribution policy

ABX Critical is a structured reference interface. Clinical recommendations remain attributable to their named guideline and page location.

## Included / derived sources

- HPUSM ASP 2025: local guide supplied for this build.
- MSIC Adult ICU 2023: official Malaysian Society of Intensive Care guide.
- MSIDC MDR Gram-Negative 2024: dedicated Malaysian MDR consensus guidance.
- Malaysia National Antimicrobial Guideline (NAG), 4th Edition: official MOH online source.
- NAG Pregnancy & Lactation appendix (Version 2, July 2024) supplied for this build.
- Wellington ICU Antibiotic Susceptibilities chart: expected-spectrum reference.

## Public GitHub packaging rule

The public PWA does not bundle the supplied copyrighted PDF files. It stores concise structured clinical data with source/page provenance and, where an official public source is available, links to that source.

NAG itself states that it is online-only and its content is subject to periodic update. The GitHub build therefore stores a topic/link index and opens the official MOH pages for current NAG content rather than redistributing the NAG website text.

## Clinical safety

Expected spectrum is not isolate susceptibility. Local epidemiology, microbiology results, infection site, source control, organ function, drug interactions, allergy history, TDM and clinician judgement remain necessary.
